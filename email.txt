## 4. Analysis of the Security Vulnerabilities on E-Mail Application

### Aim

To study and analyze the different security vulnerabilities found in e-mail applications and their prevention methods.

### Introduction

E-mail security protects messages, attachments, and personal information from unauthorized access, attacks, and misuse.

### Types of Security Vulnerabilities

1\. Phishing: Attackers send fake e-mails to trick users into revealing passwords or banking details.

2\. Spam E-mails: Unwanted e-mails may contain advertisements, harmful links, or malicious attachments.

3\. Malware Attachments: E-mail attachments may contain viruses, worms, or other malicious software.

4\. E-mail Spoofing: Attackers forge the sender's address to make an e-mail appear to come from a trusted person.

5\. Weak Passwords: Easy-to-guess passwords allow attackers to access e-mail accounts.

6\. Eavesdropping: Attackers may try to read e-mails when they are transmitted without proper encryption.

7\. Account Hijacking: Attackers gain unauthorized access to an e-mail account and misuse it.

8\. Data Leakage: Sensitive information may be exposed by sending confidential data to the wrong person or through an insecure account.

9\. Social Engineering: Attackers manipulate users into sharing confidential information or performing unsafe actions.

10\. Denial of Service (DoS): An attacker may flood an e-mail service with excessive messages or requests, disrupting its operation.

### Prevention Methods

1. Use strong and unique passwords.
2. Enable two-factor authentication (2FA).
3. Avoid opening suspicious e-mails and attachments.
4. Do not click unknown or suspicious links.
5. Use spam filters and antivirus software.
6. Use encrypted connections and e-mail encryption where appropriate.
7. Keep the e-mail application and operating system updated.
8. Verify the sender before sharing sensitive information.
9. Log out when using a shared computer.
10. Never share passwords or confidential information through suspicious e-mails.

### Result

The security vulnerabilities of e-mail applications and their prevention methods were studied.
